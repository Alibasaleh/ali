Hey — I’m Ali Basaleh.

I like making websites, poking around how systems actually work, and spending time on cyber security and technology in general. A lot of what I build is just me trying something out: a layout, a small tool, a page that feels a bit more personal than another generic portfolio.

I’m interested in web development, cyber security, and creative digital projects. I care about how things look and how they behave — buttons, pages, the little details people click without thinking about.

This site is a straightforward place to find who I am, what I’ve been working on, and how to reach me. If something’s missing, I’ll add it. If a project page still says “coming soon,” that’s because I haven’t finished writing it up yet — not because I’m hiding a secret enterprise career.

— Ali
